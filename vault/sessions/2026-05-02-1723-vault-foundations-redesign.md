# Vault Foundations Redesign — Scope/Domain, Epistemic Chain, Agents-Strategy

## Summary

The session redesigned the vault's foundational architecture across three pillars: splitting the overloaded `layer` field into a closed `scope` axis (`ontology` / `world` / `artifact`) and an open growable `domain` axis with five evidence-backed growth rules drawn from production taxonomy governance; defining the epistemic chain by which knowledge matures (research → discovery → premise → constitution → skill, with axiom as graduation target via accumulated non-falsifying evidence); and integrating the agents-strategy concept as the primary falsification signal validating premises toward axioms. Orthogonality was demoted from axiom to premise (`veracidade: low`, `convicção: high` — a "strategic bet") since the vault has no corpus instrumentation yet to measure it. Three discoveries and seven research artifacts were consolidated into `vault/discovery/vault-foundations/` with a navigation README; constitution amendments and migration plan remain queued, along with W3 (mermaid + chain refinement on `epistemic-chain.md`) and two minor edits (E1 SYNTHESIS context fix, E2 inline trigger→decision pointers).
