# Product Components Idea

## Summary

The session refined `Knowledge Calibration Geometry` from an early product framing into a more disciplined discovery with psychometric guardrails, direct-versus-inferred `C_head` evidence, anti-dashboard constraints, allowed/prohibited metric uses, smoke-test shape, and validation ladder. It then inventoried DomainSpec's product surfaces with parallel subagents and created `PRODUCT-COMPONENTS-IDEA.md` as a root-level synthesis of product lines, wedges, agents/skills, Arcanum, RAG/MCP posture, internal tools, gates, discoveries, and cross-cutting gaps. The central decision was that product metrics must be action-bearing: calibration should produce queues, diffs, evidence, and next actions before it ever becomes dashboards or people-ranking.
