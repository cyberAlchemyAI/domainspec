The Three Siblings

/research

Residue · sibling, not peer

Pre-structural work without an earned (app, feature) identity. Filed freely. Forcing it into apps/X/features/Y/ would invent identity rather than earn it.

/domain_knowledge

L₁ · structured

Specs, discoveries, domain models, invariants. Every artifact has a known (app, feature) and lives at apps/X/features/Y/{…}.

/implementation

L₂ · mirrored

Code, tests, ops. Same apps/X/features/Y/{…} grammar. Every artifact has a structurally-locatable L₁ counterpart.
