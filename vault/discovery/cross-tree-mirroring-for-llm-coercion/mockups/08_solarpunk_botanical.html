The Three Plots
🌱
/research
Residua liminalis

The seed bed. Pre-structural work. Sessions, robot-talks, unformed ideas. Not a peer layer — there is no functor leaving here. Things grow out of this plot when they earn their identity.

🌿
/domain_knowledge
L₁ structurata

The cultivated grove. Specs, invariants, domain models filed under apps/X/features/Y/. Every artifact knows its identity. This is what compilation reads from.

🌳
/implementation
L₂ speculum

The mirrored canopy. Code, tests, ops, under the same apps/X/features/Y/ grammar as L₁. Counterpart paths are computable, not searched.

Two operations. The first is identity in place — an artifact graduating because it now knows where it belongs. The second is generation in the mirror — a spec begetting its code.

Promotion 🌱→🌿

residue → L₁
Necessary: the artifact has earned an (app, feature) identity.
Sufficient: manual greenlight from the operator.
Partial: files under features/_unknown/ until the missing piece is named.

Compilation 🌿→🌳

L₁ → L₂ (Δ as Left Kan extension)
Gate: structural-completeness validator (path-coherence enforced).
Plus: test-derivability — if tests cannot be derived from the spec, the spec is underspecified.
Deferred: the formal T0' obligations from the theorem.
