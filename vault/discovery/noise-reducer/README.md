# Noise Reducer

## What is this?

A discovery folder investigating whether the human→agent interface is the most fundamental stage of specification-noise tracking (a framing the dispatch demoted, see Why it matters) — and what about the agent's partner-mandate is genuinely its own contribution versus owned prior art. The calibrated conclusion lives in [discovery.md](discovery.md); this README is navigation only.

## Business Context

Commissioned to ground a refinement of the agent's standing-instruction layer (system prompt / `CLAUDE.md`, not memory). The partner-mandate — "partner, not executor," Radical Candor, the Stop-and-Question triggers — already exists but is stated as etiquette, not defended against the sycophancy failure mode. Produced by a tensioned six-lens dispatch that dogfooded its own subject (2026-06-05).

## Why it matters

The dispatch demoted its own headline: the "first, least-recoverable gate" framing is owned prior art (Horvitz, Kim Scott, the RLHF-sycophancy literature, Boehm) plus an over-claim already operationalized by `CLAUDE.md`. What survives is the inputs the refinement actually needs — a mechanistic anti-sycophancy anchor (bind every challenge to an external referent the user did not supply), a typed noise vocabulary (Shannon-noise ≠ Kahneman-noise), and the data-processing-inequality ceiling `I(W;Y) ≤ I(W;X)` as the precise replacement for "least-recoverable." See [discovery.md](discovery.md) for the calibrated conclusion and its limits.
