# Noise Reducer — Research Synthesis

<!-- WORD BUDGET: everything below this line must be ≤500 words. -->

## Objective

Reconcile six tensioned lenses on the "first, least-recoverable noise gate" claim, separating non-owned residue from prior art restated under a new word.

## Context

A pending `research`-skill / agent-behavior refinement leans on `CLAUDE.md`'s partner-mandate, which is stated as etiquette, not defended against sycophancy. Six lenses were dispatched live (2026-06-05) as opposing angles; the skeptic killed the headline and the tension was real.

## What Was Found

- The placement claim's four clauses are **owned** — Horvitz, Kim Scott, RLHF-sycophancy, Boehm — and the conjunction is already operationalized by `CLAUDE.md`, so under cite-don't-rediscover it is restatement, not finding (`research.md#theme-d-the-demotion`).
- "Least-recoverable" is **inflated and contradicted**: the data-processing inequality `I(W;Y) ≤ I(W;X)` gives a *ceiling*, not irrecoverability, and the vault's own anti-bias file recovers bad framing downstream (`research.md#theme-d-the-demotion`).
- The noise-reducer and noise-injector are **one channel sharing parameters**; an agreement-rewarding objective makes clarifying questions a vector for user-correlated noise (`research.md#theme-b-the-reducer-is-the-injector`).
- Shannon-noise and Kahneman-noise are **two objects sharing one word**; only the Kahneman reading licenses the upstream-gate claim (`research.md#theme-c-two-different-noises`).
- Three residues survive demotion: the sycophancy paradox + external-referent defense, the typed Shannon≠Kahneman distinction, and the DPI ceiling (`research.md#unique-contributions`).

## Decisions Taken

- Adopt the external-referent anti-sycophancy rule (`../discovery.md#d-1`).
- Hold Shannon-noise and Kahneman-noise typed-distinct (`../discovery.md#d-2`).
- Replace "least-recoverable" with the DPI-ceiling form (`../discovery.md#d-3`).

## Implications

- The partner-mandate gains a mechanism (external referent) beneath its disposition, without rewriting its text.
- The noise frame is safe to use only when the two noise types are kept distinct.
- The placement is now a bounded, defensible ceiling claim rather than an over-claim.

## Open Questions

- Discovery or premise? Recommendation: likely a premise on the partner-mandate, though the sycophancy mechanism may clear the discovery bar (`../discovery.md#oq-1`).
- Is the error budget question-dominated? Recommendation: run an error-budget trace before treating the interface as highest-leverage (`../discovery.md#oq-2`).
- Can the external-referent rule be a checkable predicate, or does it collapse into cite-don't-rediscover? Recommendation: draft and test for redundancy (`../discovery.md#oq-3`).
