## E1 — Abramsky, Samson (appreciative / success-mining; sonnet; read 9 files)

The field runs ~61 daily dispatches on Lean/math; the recurring patterns are *not* math — they are dispatch-craft. Seven habits that demonstrably made real dispatches land, each general and each absent (or thin) in the generic playbook.

**1. One agent = one gate, named as a verb-noun ("precedent-kill / non-vacuity / definitional-soundness").** Real: `2026-06-12-knowledge-accumulation-bet` skeptics (Russell=precedent-kill, Bell=non-vacuity, Quine=definitional-soundness); recurs in `forced-enrichment-feasibility-2026-06-12`, `2026-06-12-pmf-equipped-site-index-escape`, `2026-06-13-residue-owned-verification`. A reusable skeptic-gate vocabulary where each gate is a *different failure mode*, not a different opinion. Manufactures tension structurally — three agents can't collapse to agreement because they aren't answering the same question. Generic says "be tensioned" but ships no standing catalogue of orthogonal gates. *Transfers:* any project can name 3 disjoint ways a claim dies (already-done / doesn't-fire / it's-a-rename).

**2. Pre-register the kill-criterion inside the sheet, then bind the headline to it ("collapse-test").** Real: `2026-06-12-tower-of-towers-feasibility` context states the collapse-test *before* agents run; `2026-06-12-vc-coupled-floor-probe` "Three collapse-tests pre-registered." Findings close with "Collapse-test (BINDS the headline)" telling the next step what verification kills the result. Pre-commits the falsifier so the parent can't move goalposts. *Transfers:* "state the single observation that would overturn this, before dispatching" — pure epistemic hygiene.

**3. Encode the bias to fight as an explicit `anti_bias_global` prior, tied to a logged wager.** Real: `2026-06-12-knowledge-accumulation-bet` — `anti_bias_global: "bettor's GO-bias (wager logged at p=0.99) vs the dispatch's owner/refuter pessimism — the dispatch exists to attack the bet, not confirm it."` Parent names its own optimism and dispatches against itself. Repeated `2026-06-13-residue-owned-verification`: "skeptics must hunt for a survivor, not confirm." *Transfers:* every team has a default-favored answer; "the owner already believes X; your job is to break X" neutralizes sponsor bias anywhere.

**4. Default-verdict assignment: give each explorer an opposite prior, not just a different topic.** Real: `2026-06-12-pmf-equipped-site-index-escape` explorers carry `"default verdict: stays-in-class vs escapes-index vs already-owned"`; `emergence-account-instantiated` gives three opposite default verdicts. Agents are told which conclusion to steelman; a writer reconciles. Covers the design space from its corners instead of drifting to a safe middle. *Transfers:* "assign each investigator the answer they must make the strongest case for" — buy/build/wait, ship/hold.

**5. Recycle preserved dissent as the seed of the next dispatch ("crack" / feedback_prompts chaining).** Real: `nonterminal-generator-tower-design` exit: "One unwitnessed crack recycled to explorer: faithful-non-full step" → spawns `faithful-nonfull-ffresidue-probe`. `vc-coupled-floor-probe` context: "Follow-up probe on crack 1 of the KILLed dispatch." The `feedback_prompts` close field carries the live handoff forward. Dissent isn't archived, it's the agenda — a KILL with one surviving crack becomes the next sheet's goal. A real, working dissent-reuse loop — the thing the playbook claims as differentiator but doesn't operationalize as a chain. *Transfers:* "the one objection nobody resolved becomes the next investigation's charter."

**6. Stable named-persona pool with role-typed reuse (Bell=non-vacuity, Leinster=precedent, Vlachopulos/Loregian=auditor).** Real: same surnames recur in the same role across dozens of dispatches; conversely `hot-rod-rig-v5.2-postfix-assessment` uses "fresh names vs both prior panels so no skeptic re-assesses a seam they previously verdicted." Personas = role-economy shorthand (name carries the gate) AND a freshness lever (rotate to defeat anchoring). Generic has `agent-pool.yaml` but no doctrine of role-stable-yet-rotatable naming. *Transfers:* a fixed cast where each name = a fixed lens, plus "new names for a re-review."

**7. The auditor's job is a specific false-consensus trigger ("flag if N≥3 returned zero dissent").** Real: every auditor prompt carries it; `surprisal` close fired it as earned-convergence-not-suppressed; `pmf-equipped-site-index-escape` sharpens it: "check whether G1/G2/G3 all collapse by the SAME mechanism … if so mark SINGLE-MECHANISM (weaker negative)." Makes "did the panel actually disagree?" a falsifiable post-condition. *Transfers:* "auto-flag any panel of 3+ that produced zero dissent, and downgrade a conclusion whose every argument shares one mechanism."

**Honorable mention (evidence-citation):** findings anchors every verdict to `file:line` + named theorem; close-rows confess process failures ("Agent candidates proved UNRELIABLE — every CT file needed parent build-fix rounds"). The close row records process failures, not just results — telemetry that admits "the agents were wrong here."

**Dissent:** E2 will argue #2 (collapse-test) and #7 (single-mechanism flag) lean on the field's binary checkable outcome (Lean compiles or not) and degrade to theater in a fuzzy domain; may call #6 cargo-cult. E3 will contest attribution: #1 (gate triad) and #4 (opposite verdicts) are already partially in the generic anti-bias/check-tension machinery, failing the "not already here" bar; her sharper picks will be #3 and #5.

## E2 — Bell, John S. (adversarial / friction-mining; sonnet; read 8 files)

**1. Writer + auditor roles "folded into the parent for spend" so routinely the 4-role cadence is aspirational.** Real: `2026-06-13-residue-closure-assessment` close (line 725) "Writer/auditor roles folded into parent synthesis for spend; read-only research agents returned inline rather than writing per-agent files"; same in `fullness-residue-presheaf` (744), `residue-count-escape-fifth` (933 "Writer + auditor are parent-enacted"), structured-args (391). Two of four "epistemic functions" are in practice the parent doing synthesis. **CUT:** bless "parent-enacted writer/auditor" as a first-class named shape (`parent-synthesis` mode), not a degraded fallback; writer/auditor-as-child is the opt-in exception for high-stakes artifacts.

**2. Per-agent provenance files (`agents/NN-role-surname.md`) abandoned constantly — agents "returned inline."** Real: lines 725, 744 ("returned inline rather than writing per-agent files"), 1024, 70 ("all per-agent files materialized by parent verbatim"). Read-only children can't write the tree; the parent transcribes or skips the layer. **CUT:** collapse the mandatory `agents/` layer into an optional inline-returns mode; keep one synthesis doc as load-bearing; per-agent files opt-in where provenance is contested.

**3. Robot-talks audits keep shrinking rosters — the 7-agent template is over-built.** Real: `residue-count-escape-fifth` (933) "Lean 3-agent variant resized from 7 per the 2026-06-15 robot-talks audit"; full 7–8-agent dispatches coexist with a flood of 2–4-agent ones. An explicit audit had to cut a live dispatch nearly in half. **CUT/ADD:** tiered default where the minimal shape (1 explorer + 2 tensioned skeptics, parent-synthesized) is recommended; full 7-role roster reserved for triangulation/adversarial-audit tiers.

**4. Background-agent process death with silent data loss — no retry/checkpoint primitive.** Real: `residue-substantive-build` `exit_reason: error` (947–949) "Both build agents lost to a Claude Code process exit before writing any file… Re-launched #2 only"; `residue-substantive-build-retry` (950) is a whole second row that exists only to recover; Zulip build (1000) same shape. **ADD:** a dispatch-completion/liveness invariant — parent-side "did the child produce its artifact?" check + a typed `relaunch_of:` linkage so retries are recorded as continuations, not new dispatches polluting the ledger.

**5. Agents return guessed/fabricated specifics the parent must catch — output isn't load-bearing without a verify gate.** Real: line 541 "Agent-supplied headline decl names were guesses; parent extracts real ones"; 559 (tier-inflation, parent rejected); 409 "Bell caught a factual error propagated from the bridge/harvest-E1/writer… Three artifacts corrected." Tensioning catches *bias*; plain *fabrication* needs a separate verify-on-disk gate. **ADD:** an explicit "claims-anchored-to-artifact, verified before propagation" gate distinct from the skeptic gates.

**6. The typed `exit_reason` enum didn't fit early reality — free-text leaked until a schema migration.** Real: first ~13 dispatches (no `schema_version`) use exit reasons outside the enum — "synthesis_complete" (26), "success" (69) — while everything from line 118 is `0.5.2` with typed reasons. The original enum had no slot for the most common real outcome ("the panel adjudicated and I'm done"). **ADD:** typed `exit_reason` must include an `adjudicated`/`resolved` terminal; validate enums against a real run-log before freezing.

**Note on what didn't strain:** `max_loops`=1 on the overwhelming majority; `loops_used>1` appears only twice (a deliberate round-2 re-review); the loop_cap machinery never hit its ceiling. The multi-loop / `loop_cap` apparatus is near-dead machinery in real use.

**Dissent:** E1 will read "folded for spend" / "parent-enacted" as evidence the roles are robust enough to compress (the framework working under budget pressure) — keep them as *roles* regardless of who enacts them. E3 will reframe my #3 (over-built roster) and #6 (enum migration) as healthy evolution visible only by comparing old free-form rows to `0.5.2` rows — not gaps to fix but proof the version delta is the lesson. I mine the friction as a standing defect; E3 mines the same delta as a successful migration trajectory.

## E3 — Quine, Willard Van Orman (comparative / ruleset-diff; opus; read 13 files)

**Delta 1 — Dedicated agent *types* on disk vs role-as-a-field.** Field: four roles shipped as real `.claude/agents/research-{explorer,skeptic,writer,auditor}.md` with locked `tools:`/`model:` frontmatter and hard-wired posture — skeptic "Your default posture is hostile: assume each candidate is wrong until it survives your attack"; explorer read-only by construction. Generic: role is a per-agent free-text field filled into `initial_prompt`; "the strategist may write findings.md itself or delegate (no mandatory writer-agent machinery)." Direction: field-has-it-generic-lacks. *Change:* the locked read-only + hostile-posture frontmatter is a general safety guarantee prose can't enforce (a strategist can forget "read-only" or soften the skeptic). Generic could ship 3-4 reusable role agent-type stubs with frozen tools/posture.

**Delta 2 — Per-folder roster (`dispatch.yaml`) vs single append-only ledger.** Field: "dispatch.yaml is the roster" — each topic materializes `<corpus>/<topic-slug>/dispatch.yaml` co-located with its `agents/` and `findings.md`. Generic: one global ledger, two appends, working artifacts in a separate `working_folder`. Direction: cuts both ways (single ledger buys cross-dispatch telemetry; field buys locality). *Change:* the general win is **co-locating the anti_bias/tension declaration with the findings it governs** — generic's `anti_bias_global` lives in the ledger row while findings live in `working_folder`, so a findings reader can't see the tension design without opening a second file. Mirror the roster/anti_bias summary into the working_folder.

**Delta 3 — Anti-bias lifecycle: init-only vs init AND post-dispatch false-consensus flag.** Field: enforced both ends; post-dispatch — "If a layer of N≥3 returns zero dissent and all findings agree, treat it as a failure to exercise the tensioning, NOT success… The auditor fires this flag. Every explorer/skeptic ends with an explicit Dissent: line so this check has signal." Generic: init-only, explicitly — check-tension "There is no post-dispatch realization check — the Dissent:-line apparatus was retired." Direction: field-has-it-generic-lacks, but generic *deliberately removed* it (contested). *Change:* the claim is general — designing tension doesn't prove it was exercised; N≥3 unanimity with zero dissent is a measurable failure signal. Restore a lightweight post-hoc false-consensus check as a **final_approver/auditor** responsibility (costs no new machinery — generic already has a final_approver).

**Delta 4 — Tension definition: identical core, but field carries the green-light sentence as a hard authoring step.** Field: in the dispatching skill itself, "for every explorer pair you must be able to write the sentence 'a_i runs [value_i], a_j runs [value_j] on the [axis] axis; a bias in a_i would be exposed by a_j' … else reject (VC-G1)." Generic: same sentence/axes but behind the two-agent check-tension Test 4, referenced as P5 in the router. *Change:* the field proves the predicted-disagreement sentence is usable as a one-person authoring check — suggesting generic's two-independent-agent gate may be over-built for the common small dispatch.

**Delta 5 — Lifecycle weight: parent-enacted validator vs two-agent check-tension gate + dedicated final_approver.** Field: "The validator (the '+1' meta-role) is parent-enacted, not a child agent: before any dispatch you check the dispatch.yaml yourself … and run the tensioning check. If it fails, fix it before launching." One person, inline, no extra dispatch. Generic: two independent spawned agents (checker + reviewer) PLUS a separate final_approver PLUS the four-step lifecycle PLUS three loop dials. Direction: generic-over-complicates. *Change:* the field runs ~61 real dispatches with a self-applied validator and no tensioning sub-dispatch; for the common small research dispatch the two-agent check-tension gate is heavier than the failure it prevents. Make check-tension **scale with sheet size** — parent self-check for single subject-group sheets, the two-agent gate for meta/large fan-outs.

**Delta 6 — Robot-talks: standalone audit skill vs a per-group boolean.** Field: robot-talks is its own skill with a 4-phase lifecycle, its own human gate, decompose "by concerns, not files. No two agents investigate the same question," and a mandatory per-agent report format (Key Findings / Gaps / Local Tensions / Questions-for-Synthesis). Generic: `robot_talks: true` per-group boolean binding the robot-talks constitution, single-gate override. *Change:* lift the field's "concerns-not-files + 4-part report format" into the generic research skill's robot-talks branch as the synthesizer's input contract.

**Dissent:** E1 will contest Delta 1's direction — generic's role-as-a-field is the deliberately more-general design; four frozen agent-type files are the field over-fitting to its four math roles. E2 will contest Delta 3 and Delta 5 hardest — defending generic's init-only anti-bias and two-agent gate as a considered 2026-06-15 owner decision, calling "restore false-consensus flag" a regression, and arguing the two-agent gate's independence is exactly what the field's single self-checking parent lacks (inverting Delta 5). Sharpest three-way fight: is the field's lighter lifecycle *evidence it's sufficient* (my read, 61 live dispatches) or *evidence it's under-governed and merely hasn't failed yet* (E2's read)?
