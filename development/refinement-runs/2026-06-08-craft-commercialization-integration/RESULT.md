## Verdict in one line

**Craft is the right-shaped governance product for this plan — but today it's free infrastructure, and its entire paid value lives in the parts it has explicitly deferred.** It is the _lever_ for R2, not the _closure_ of R2.

## How Craft integrates (the three convergent findings)

1. **Structurally it replaces nothing — it's a separable, ship-now free ledger that composes the rest (Cartographer).** Craft is a recursive governance ledger (`.craft/ledger.yml` + Markdown view) with zero hard dependency on other Arcanum capabilities; the `SKILL.md` is already a portable contract designed to drop into another repo. It routes to invoke/refine/task-session and ingests their receipts; it does not execute or replace them. Promotion is **deferred** (readiness doc says `defer`; the active validation reports `block` on incomplete Distill).

2. **On the free/paid boundary it's open-core the _right way around_ (Linker).** Free = the ledger schema, format, type/lane system, blocker-refinement discipline (a strong wedge that makes the existing free wedge stickier with a governance trail). Paid = the **deferred** automation — role-delegation, priority scoring, generated index, hosted reflection — plus attested ledger templates for the regulated vertical. This directly fixes the prior run's "open-core inversion" risk: free format, paid governed loop.

3. **But the paid moat is unbuilt, and the shipped asset is a forkable YAML file (Adversary).** Only two features are genuinely novel — the enforced blocker-refinement gate and residue-as-first-class — and both are process-shaped (replicable as a competitor automation rule) and currently manual. A portable YAML+Markdown ledger has no data gravity, no network effect, no enforcement, no metering. Default verdict: **free dev-infrastructure**, paid only under five conditions that are _all currently deferred or unbuilt_.

## Answers to the live questions

- **Is Craft the R2 lever (productize governance, escape consulting)? → PARTIAL.** It is exactly the self-serve governance _surface_ R2 demanded, and it manufactures value-aligned usage metrics (governed contexts, refined blockers, recompositions) instead of seats. But the value-_metric_ and _enforcement_ are the deferred pieces. Craft converts R2 from "open product question" into a concrete build order: **ship the deferred automation as the paid meter.**
- **Relationship to the CyberAlchemy promotion DAG? → COMPOSE, not compete.** Craft is the _work/governance_ ledger; the DAG is the _knowledge-trust_ ledger. Clean seam: a Craft decision/definition emits a PromotionRecord draft → the DAG adjudicates → promoted policies feed back as Craft gates. Package both = the moat; they are two halves of one governed lifecycle.
- **Does it touch R1 (engine determinism)? → It hardens, does not fix.** Craft makes every spec→test run leave an immutable, auditable governance trail = R1's "re-anchor on the proprietary governance-data asset" escape. It does _not_ make the engine deterministic — Lean remains that lever. Craft (process record) + Lean (proof) are complementary.
