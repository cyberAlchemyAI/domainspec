## Objective Summary

- Objective: deliver (1) an MVP fully-deterministic test-derivation engine that makes C2 determinism true by construction, and (2) the reframed E1a/E2/E3 experiments that validate derived-test quality on top of it.
- Primary inputs: [../RESULT.md](../RESULT.md), [../stages/architect-deterministic-engine.md](../stages/architect-deterministic-engine.md), [../stages/cartographer-measurement-and-infra-map.md](../stages/cartographer-measurement-and-infra-map.md), poker-team feature docs + backend.
- Success condition: engine round-trips `financial-settlement` TEST-SPEC byte-stably (L0); reframed experiments produce pre-registered, non-contaminated results in `docs/research/results/` (L2–L3).
