It is not: the stated bound uses only the **global** `max_loops`. The per-edge caps and the sequential-vs-feedback typing are **decoration that does not appear in the multiplicand.** Empirically this is forced: every `connections` entry in the registry is `type:"sequential"`; the lone `max_loops:3` dispatch carries `connections: []` and looped by global re-fan (`feedback_prompts`), never via a typed back-edge with its own cap. So on the actual witness the edge-type field is never read by the measure.

Therefore the measure IS the termination measure of bounded iteration; the typed-edge DAG is presentation, not content of the bound. The writer's own collapse-test is satisfied — concede it.
